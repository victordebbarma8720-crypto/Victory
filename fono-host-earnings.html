FONO Host Earnings and Salary Structure

Video and audio call rates for Levels 1–6, the salary table, worked per-minute examples and the factors that influence a host’s earnings.

Published 2 November 20267 min readBased on the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025

How much can a FONO host earn? The honest answer is: it depends on your level, how many minutes of calls you take, the gifts you receive and your performance. What the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025 does publish is the rate per minute at each level and the salary conversion. This article puts those numbers together.

Video call earnings by level

Video calls are the higher-paying call type. The rate rises by 100 Beans per minute at each level, from 100 at Level 1 to 600 at Level 6.

Audio call earnings by level

Audio calls pay a flat 100 Beans per minute for Levels 1 to 4, then 150 at Level 5 and 200 at Level 6.

Worked examples

  • Level 1, video: 60 minutes × 100 Beans = 6,000 Beans ≈ ₹60.
  • Level 4, video: 60 minutes × 400 Beans = 24,000 Beans ≈ ₹240.
  • Level 6, audio: 60 minutes × 200 Beans = 12,000 Beans ≈ ₹120.

These are illustrations of the published rates, not predictions. Real call minutes vary from day to day.

What affects a host’s earnings?

Your level

Higher levels earn more per minute, especially on video calls.

Call flow and Streamer Score

The policy states that weekly earnings and performance influence call flow and call rates. Read FONO Streamer Score explained.

Virtual gifts

Gifts add Beans on top of call earnings; check gift values in the app.

Eligibility

Only eligible earnings are credited and withdrawable, and correct PAN details are needed for withdrawals.

Actual earnings, commission and withdrawals are subject to platform eligibility and the rules in force. No income is guaranteed, and not every host reaches the higher call rates.

To turn your balance into rupees, see the FONO Beans to INR guide and the withdrawal policy.
