Overview

What Is FONO Hosting?

FONO Hosting means taking video calls and audio calls from users on the FONO app and receiving virtual gifts, in return for Beans. The platform uses a level-based earning structure, meaning applicable call rates vary depending on the streamer’s level.

This guide explains Fono Host Registration, earning rates, salary conversion, Streamer Score and withdrawal requirements, all based on the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025.

How to become a Fono Host – step by step

Video calls

100–600 Beans per minute, depending on level.

Audio calls

100–200 Beans per minute, depending on level.

Virtual gifts

Gifts from users add Beans to your balance.

Self-withdrawal

Withdraw every day, 8 AM – 8 PM.

Registration

Fono Host Registration Requirements

The supplied policy keeps host registration simple. You do not need to prepare photos or record an audition. What you do need is accurate identity information for withdrawals.

  • No profile picture required according to the supplied policy.
  • No audition video required according to the supplied policy.
  • Personal PAN card number required.
  • Incorrect PAN details may prevent future Bean withdrawals.

PAN Card Requirements

FONO requires your personal PAN card number – not a family member’s, a friend’s or your agent’s.

  • Enter the 10-character PAN exactly as printed on your card.
  • Double-check letters and numbers before you submit.
  • Incorrect PAN details may block future Bean withdrawals.
  • Never share your PAN with anyone except the official registration process.

Virtual Gifts

Virtual Gifts

Besides per-minute call earnings, FONO users can send virtual gifts to streamers. Gifts are one of the activities through which eligible streamers earn Beans. The supplied policy does not publish a separate Beans value for each gift, so check the gift values shown in the app.

Salary

FONO Host Salary Structure

FONO converts the Beans a streamer has earned into a rupee salary at a fixed ratio. The policy publishes the following salary points – each one is simply the Beans total converted at 1,000 Beans = ₹10.

Beans to INR Conversion

1,000 Beans = ₹10

That means 100 Beans = ₹1. To convert any Beans balance to rupees, divide it by 100. For example, 250,000 Beans ÷ 100 = ₹2,500.

Withdrawal

FONO Host Withdrawal Policy

FONO streamers withdraw their own Beans (self-withdrawal). The policy lists 5,000 Beans for your first withdrawal (≈ ₹50) and 20,000 Beans for each subsequent withdrawal (≈ ₹200). Withdrawals are available every day between 8 AM and 8 PM.

Your PAN details must be correct for withdrawals to go through. The policy does not specify a time zone for the withdrawal window, so follow the timing shown in the app.

FAQ

Fono Hosting Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a profile picture or audition video to become a Fono Host?

No. According to the supplied policy, no profile picture and no audition video are required to register as a host.

What document do I need for Fono Host Registration?

You need your personal PAN card number. Incorrect PAN details may prevent future Bean withdrawals.

How much does a Fono Host earn per minute?

It depends on your level. Video calls earn 100 Beans/min at Level 1 up to 600 Beans/min at Level 6. Audio calls earn 100 Beans/min at Levels 1–4, 150 at Level 5 and 200 at Level 6.

What is 1,000 FONO Beans in rupees?

Under the policy, 1,000 Beans = ₹10. So 100,000 Beans = ₹1,000 and 1,000,000 Beans = ₹10,000.

When can I withdraw my Beans?

Streamers use self-withdrawal every day between 8 AM and 8 PM. The policy lists 5,000 Beans for the first withdrawal and 20,000 Beans for subsequent withdrawals.

What is a good Streamer Score on FONO?

The policy states that the average app Streamer Score is 70, and the top 50 hosts have scores above 90. Weekly earnings and performance influence call flow and call rates.

Will I always get the Level 6 rate?

No. Rates depend on your level and performance. Not every host reaches the higher levels, and no earnings are guaranteed.
