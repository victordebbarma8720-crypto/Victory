Overview

What Is a Chamet Agency?

A Chamet agency is a team leader on the platform. The agency finds suitable hosts, helps them register and verify their accounts, explains the platform rules and supports them as they grow.

In return, the platform pays the agency a commission on the cashable earnings of its team. This commission is paid separately from the hosts' salaries – an agency never takes money from a host's earnings.

Agencies can also invite sub-agencies. The parent agency then earns the difference between its own commission ratio and the sub-agency's ratio.

Responsibilities

Structure

Commission Structure

How the platform calculates agency commission, based on the supplied platform presentation.

  • Direct hosts: cashable earnings × your ratio.
  • Sub-agencies: (your ratio − sub-agency ratio) × their cashable earnings.
  • If a sub-agency has the same ratio as you, your commission from that sub-agency is $0.
  • Your daily ratio depends on eligible team earnings over the recent 30 days.
  • New agents (under 30 days) may be measured from registration date to the previous day.
  • Refunds through Google, Apple or other methods may be deducted from related earnings.

Rules

Chamet Agency Rules

Breaking these rules can lead to restrictions, suspension or loss of commission.

Agency must

  • Follow all platform policies and instructions.
  • Let the platform pay hosts directly – agency commissions are paid separately.
  • Register only eligible adults who are new to Chamet agencies.
  • Keep communication professional and respectful.

Agency must not

  • Register hostesses who already work with another Chamet agency.

FAQ

Chamet Agency FAQs

Common questions about agency registration, commission and payments.

What is a Chamet agency?

A Chamet agency is a partner that recruits, onboards and supports hosts on the Chamet app. The platform pays the agency a commission based on the eligible earnings of its team, separately from the hosts' own salaries.

How do I get the Chamet agency registration link?

The agency registration link is provided separately by the platform. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share the current link and help you complete the registration.

Is there a fee to register as a Chamet agency?

We do not charge for information or for sharing the registration link. If anyone asks you for money to 'guarantee' approval or income, be careful and confirm directly with the platform.

How is agency commission calculated?

For your direct hosts, commission is their cashable earnings multiplied by your ratio. For a sub-agency, you earn the difference between your ratio and theirs on their cashable earnings, for example (22% - 10%) x $100 = $12.

How is my commission ratio decided?

Your daily ratio depends on your team's eligible earnings over the recent 30 days. New agents with less than 30 days of activity may be measured from their registration date through the previous day. Confirm current ratio levels on the platform.

Where do I log in to the agency dashboard?

The agency dashboard login page is newa4mnou8u5xdi9.rchamet.com/user/login/. Use the login details given to you after approval and never share your password.

When is agency commission paid?

Agency commission is paid separately from hostess salaries, following the platform's daily settlement rules. Withdrawals start at $10 and are made in multiples of $10 to your bound Metwallet account.

Can I recruit hosts who already work with another agency?

No. Registering hostesses who already work with another Chamet agency, or soliciting them to switch, is against the rules and can lead to restrictions or loss of commission.

Do I need experience to start an agency?

No formal experience is required, but you should be comfortable recruiting people, explaining rules, and supporting your team professionally. We share guidance when you contact us.
