How to Join Chamet as a Host in India

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

Joining Chamet as a host is straightforward if you prepare properly. This guide walks you through each step so you can start streaming with a verified, rule-compliant account.

Step 1: Check that you are eligible

You must be 18 or older, use only one personal Chamet account and not already be working with another Chamet agency. You also need a smartphone, stable internet and good lighting.

Step 2: Install the app and create your account

Install Chamet on your own phone and create a single account. Multiple accounts are prohibited and can lead to suspension.

Step 3: Verify your identity

Complete face verification and bind your own phone number or Gmail account. These steps are required before you can withdraw earnings.

Step 4: Get the current host registration link

Host registration links can change, so we share the current link on WhatsApp. Message us and we will help you join under an agency.

Step 5: Set up Metwallet and your profile

Bind a valid Metwallet account for withdrawals, then upload a clear, high-definition photo with good lighting, a tidy background and appropriate clothing.

For full details, read our Chamet host registration page.

How to Become a Chamet Agency Partner

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

A Chamet agency recruits and supports hosts and receives a commission from the platform based on its team's eligible earnings. Here is how to get started.

Is an agency right for you?

Agencies suit people who enjoy building teams, explaining rules clearly and supporting others. You will spend time recruiting eligible hosts, helping them set up and checking in regularly.

How registration works

The platform provides the agency registration link separately. Message us on WhatsApp to request the current link. Prepare your own Chamet account, bind your phone or Gmail and set up a valid Metwallet account.

After approval

Once approved, you log in to the agency dashboard to follow your team, earnings and commission ratio. Keep your login private and always check the address before signing in.

Start with good habits

Recruit only adults who are new to Chamet agencies, never deduct host payments, and follow every platform instruction. These rules protect your commission.

Earnings

Understanding the Chamet Earning System

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

Chamet's earning system has two sides: host earnings and agency commission. Both are controlled by platform rules.

How hosts earn

Hosts receive Beans from eligible virtual gifts during live streams and from private video calls. Beans are the earning unit inside the app.

From Beans to money

The platform converts Beans into a withdrawable balance at its current conversion rate. Because the rate can change, always check it in the app rather than relying on figures you see online.

How agencies earn

Agencies earn a daily commission ratio on their team's cashable earnings. This is paid separately by the platform and never taken out of a host's salary.

What affects results

Actual earnings depend on platform rules, activity, eligibility, performance and any refunds or deductions. There is no guaranteed income on Chamet.

Chamet Agency Commission Explained

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

Chamet agency commission is calculated daily. Understanding the formula helps you plan how to grow your team.

Direct hosts

For hosts registered directly under you, commission = their cashable earnings × your ratio. Example: $1,500 × 22% = $330.

Sub-agencies

For a sub-agency, you earn the difference between your ratio and theirs. Example: (22% − 10%) × $100 = $12, and (22% − 18%) × $500 = $20. If both ratios are 22%, your commission from that sub-agency is $0.

How the ratio is set

Your daily ratio depends on eligible team earnings over the recent 30 days. New agents with less than 30 days may be measured from their registration date through the previous day.

The worked example

In the supplied presentation, Agent A (22%) earns $12 + $20 + $0 + $330 = $362 for the day. This is an illustrative example, not a guaranteed amount.

Rules

Chamet Host Rules and Guidelines

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

Following the rules is the simplest way to protect your account and your earnings. Violations may lead to restrictions, suspension or loss of related earnings and commissions.

Account rules

  • Multiple accounts are prohibited.
  • Users under 18 are not eligible.
  • Switching agencies through improper solicitation is prohibited.

Content rules

  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content is prohibited.
  • Avoid revealing clothing and vulgar poses.
  • Do not lie down or show a bed during live streams.

Behaviour on live and calls

  • Keep your face visible and use proper lighting.
  • Be respectful to every viewer and caller.
  • Follow platform warnings and AI inspection requirements immediately.
  • Do not move users to other applications.

How to Improve Your Chamet Host Profile

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

A strong profile helps you get approved and noticed. Chamet checks profile quality, so small improvements matter.

Use a clear, HD photo

Take your photo in daylight or with a ring light. Make sure your face is sharp, well lit and not hidden by heavy filters.

Choose a tidy background

A plain wall or neat room looks professional. Remove clutter and avoid showing a bed.

Dress decently

Wear appropriate, comfortable clothing. Revealing outfits and vulgar poses are not allowed and can cause your profile to be rejected.

Write a friendly bio

Share your interests and the languages you speak. Do not add contact details for other apps.

Keep it updated

Refresh your photo occasionally and keep it in line with the standards so returning viewers recognise you.

Payments

Chamet Withdrawal and Payment Guidelines

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

These are the settlement rules described in the supplied Chamet platform presentation. Always confirm the latest rules in the app.

Requirements

  • Complete face verification.
  • Bind your own phone number or Gmail account.
  • Bind a valid Metwallet account.

How withdrawals work

  • Yesterday's salary can be withdrawn under the daily settlement process.
  • The minimum withdrawal is $10, in multiples of $10.
  • Any remainder accumulates toward your next withdrawal.
  • The platform presentation states that eligible withdrawals reach the bound Metwallet in real time.

Deductions and separate payments

Refunds through Google, Apple or other payment methods may be deducted from related hostess earnings. Host salaries and agency commissions are paid separately – an agency should never collect or hold a host's payment.

How to Build a Successful Chamet Agency Team

Chamet Agency India · Updated 31 October 2026

A successful agency is built on trust. Hosts stay with agencies that are honest, helpful and fair.

Recruit the right people

Look for confident, friendly adults with time to go live regularly. Check that they are 18+, have only one account and are not already with another Chamet agency.

Onboard properly

Help each new host with face verification, phone or Gmail binding, Metwallet setup and a quality profile photo.

Explain the rules early

Share the content, profile and live standards before the first stream. Most problems come from rules nobody explained.

Monitor and support

Use the agency dashboard to follow activity and earnings, then share specific, kind feedback. Celebrate progress.

Grow with sub-agencies

Invite capable team leaders as sub-agencies. Remember that you earn the ratio difference, so a fair structure benefits everyone.

Never cut corners

No poaching, no moving hosts to other apps and never withholding payments. Breaking rules risks your whole team's commission.
