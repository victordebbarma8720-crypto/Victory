Overview

What Is FONO Agency?

Through the FONO agency structure, registered agencies can receive commission based on the eligible earnings generated by their associated streamers.

An agency’s job is to find suitable streamers, help them register correctly, and support them so they stay active and follow FONO policies. The agency does not take calls itself – its income comes from the 10% commission on its streamers’ eligible earnings.

Commission

FONO Agency Commission Structure

According to the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025, FONO pays registered agencies a flat agency commission of 10%. The commission is based on the eligible earnings of the streamers registered under the agency, and the minimum agency withdrawal is 100,000 Beans.

How FONO Agency Commission Works

Each time a registered streamer earns eligible Beans from calls or gifts, 10% of that amount counts toward the agency’s commission. Because the rate is flat, an agency grows its commission by growing the number of active, rule-compliant streamers – not by reaching a higher tier.

Only eligible earnings count. Earnings that the platform reverses, disqualifies or does not credit are not a basis for commission.

Illustrative calculation

100,000 Beans × 10% = 10,000 Beans

If eligible streamer earnings total 100,000 Beans, a 10% commission would represent 10,000 Beans. This is an illustrative calculation, not a guaranteed payable commission. The platform calculates the actual amount.

Withdrawal

Minimum Agency Withdrawal

Agency commission can be withdrawn once the agency balance reaches 100,000 Beans. Using the policy’s conversion of 1,000 Beans = ₹10, 100,000 Beans corresponds to ₹1,000. The supplied policy states this conversion in the streamer salary section, so confirm the rate that applies to agency withdrawals in your agency account.

With a flat 10% commission, reaching 100,000 Beans in commission means the agency’s streamers have generated about 1,000,000 Beans of eligible earnings in total.

Withdrawals are subject to platform eligibility, verification and the rules in force at the time. Penalties for rule violations can be deducted from agency commission.

Rules & Regulations

FONO Agency Rules and Regulations

Recruitment rules listed in the supplied policy. They exist to stop agencies poaching streamers from each other.

Rule 1

No Cross-Agency Recruitment Verify

Agents must not recruit streamers from other agents. The source policy states this rule for “Kandy”.

Rule 2

No Promotion of Other Platforms Verify

Promoting other platforms is prohibited. The source policy states this rule as “on Kandy”.

Rule 3

One Agent Per Streamer Verify

A streamer cannot join multiple agents simultaneously.

Rule 4

No Recruitment of Existing Platform Hosts Verify

Agents must not recruit existing hosts from other agencies on the platform.

Penalties

Agency Violation Penalties

The supplied policy sets escalating penalties for agencies that break the recruitment rules. Penalties are charged in Beans, and repeated violations can end the agency relationship altogether.

Because a single first-offence penalty (100,000 Beans) equals the entire minimum withdrawal amount, one violation can wipe out a significant share of an agency’s commission.

Recruitment

Recruitment Guidelines

Good practice for building a healthy, compliant streamer network.

Do

  • Recruit new streamers who are not registered with any other agent.
  • Explain the real call rates and that earnings are not guaranteed.
  • Make sure each streamer enters their own, correct PAN card number.
  • Share the withdrawal thresholds (5,000 Beans first, 20,000 Beans after) and timings.
  • Keep records of who you recruited and when.

Don’t

  • Approach hosts already working with another agency.
  • Ask a streamer to register with more than one agent.
  • Promote other platforms to FONO users or streamers.
  • Promise fixed income or the maximum call rate.
  • Ask streamers for money to register.
FAQ

Fono Agency Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FONO agency commission rate?

The FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025 sets a flat agency commission of 10%, calculated on the eligible earnings of the agency’s registered streamers.

What is the minimum withdrawal for a Fono Agency?

Agency commission can be withdrawn once it reaches 100,000 Beans, according to the supplied policy.

Can a streamer join two FONO agencies?

No. The policy states one agent per streamer – a streamer cannot join multiple agents at the same time.

Can I recruit hosts who already work with another agency?

No. The policy prohibits recruiting existing hosts from other agencies on the platform and recruiting streamers from other agents. These rules are pending confirmation for FONO (see the note above), so check with your agency manager.

What happens if an agency breaks the rules?

The policy lists a 100,000 Beans penalty for a first offence, 500,000 Beans for a second offence, and commission deductions and possible termination of agency cooperation for repeated violations.

Is the 10% commission guaranteed?

No. Commission is calculated on eligible streamer earnings and is subject to platform eligibility and rules. Examples on this page are illustrations only.

Do I pay a fee to register as a Fono Agency?

The supplied policy does not mention any registration fee. Be cautious of anyone asking for money to register, and confirm the official process before sharing personal details.
