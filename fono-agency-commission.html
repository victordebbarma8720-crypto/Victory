FONO Agency Commission Explained

The flat 10% rate, what counts as eligible earnings, the 100,000 Beans withdrawal minimum, worked examples and how penalties reduce commission.

Published 2 November 20266 min readBased on the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025

The FONO agency commission is one of the simplest in the live-app industry: a single flat rate with no tiers. This article explains how it is calculated, when you can withdraw it and what can reduce it, based on the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025.

The rate: a flat 10%

FONO agency commission at a glance
ParticularsDetails
Agency Commission10%
Minimum Withdrawal100,000 Beans
Commission BasisRegistered Streamer Earnings

Every registered agency receives 10% of the eligible earnings of its registered streamers. Whether an agency has five streamers or five hundred, the percentage is the same; the total grows only with the streamers’ eligible earnings.

What counts as “eligible” earnings?

Commission is based on what streamers actually earn and the platform credits – for example Beans from video calls, audio calls and virtual gifts. Earnings that the platform does not credit, reverses or disqualifies do not create commission. The platform calculates the final figure.

Worked examples

The policy example

If eligible streamer earnings total 100,000 Beans, a 10% commission would represent 10,000 Beans. This is an illustrative calculation, not guaranteed payable commission.

Minimum agency withdrawal: 100,000 Beans

An agency can withdraw commission once it reaches 100,000 Beans. As the table shows, that requires about 1,000,000 Beans of eligible earnings across your streamers. Growing a network of active streamers is therefore the key to regular agency withdrawals.

How penalties affect commission

Agency violation penalties
ViolationPenalty
First Offence100,000 Beans
Second Offence500,000 Beans
Repeated ViolationsCommission deductions and possible termination of agency cooperation

A single first-offence penalty equals the whole minimum withdrawal. Read FONO agency rules and regulations so you never lose commission to an avoidable violation.

Actual earnings, commission and withdrawals are subject to platform eligibility and the rules in force. No income is guaranteed, and not every host reaches the higher call rates.
