Earnings

Host Earning Opportunities

Chamet hosts can earn in a few different ways. All earnings are credited according to platform rules.

Live streaming

Go live and interact with viewers. Eligible virtual gifts are credited to you as Beans.

Private video calls

Accept one-to-one video calls. Eligible call time is credited as Beans.

Gifts & interactions

Friendly, engaging hosts usually receive more gifts and repeat callers.

Flexible schedule

Choose your own hours. Regular, consistent activity helps build an audience.

No guaranteed income. Earnings depend on platform rules, your activity, eligibility and performance, and on refunds or deductions applied by the platform.

Live Streaming Guidelines

  • Keep your face clearly visible throughout the stream.
  • Use proper lighting and a tidy background.
  • Wear decent, appropriate clothing – no revealing outfits or vulgar poses.
  • Sit upright; avoid lying down or showing a bed.
  • Use earphones where appropriate for clear sound.
  • Respond to platform warnings and AI inspection immediately.

Video Call Guidelines

  • Answer calls in a quiet, well-lit place.
  • Be polite, friendly and respectful to every caller.
  • Keep your face in frame and your camera steady.
  • Never share pornographic or sexually explicit content.
  • Do not move callers to other apps or ask for outside payments.
  • End and report calls that break the rules.

Profile

Profile Requirements

Your profile is the first thing viewers see. Chamet checks profiles for quality.

  • A clear, high-definition profile photograph of yourself.
  • Proper lighting and good image brightness.
  • A tidy, uncluttered background.
  • Decent and appropriate clothing.
  • No revealing clothing, vulgar poses or heavy filters that hide your face.
  • A friendly, honest bio without contact details for other apps.
Eligibility

Eligibility Requirements

You can join as a Chamet host if you meet all of the following.

  • You are 18 years or older.
  • You use one personal account only.
  • You are not already working with another Chamet agency.
  • You can complete face verification.
  • You can bind your own phone number or Gmail account.
  • You have, or can create, a valid Metwallet account.
  • You have a smartphone and a stable internet connection.

Rules

Chamet Host Rules

Policy violations may result in account restrictions, suspension or loss of related earnings and commissions.

  • Multiple accounts are prohibited.
  • Switching agencies through improper solicitation is prohibited.
  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content is prohibited.
  • Users under 18 are not eligible.
  • Do not move users or hosts to other applications.
Tips

Tips to Improve Your Performance

Small habits that help hosts build a loyal audience.

  • Go live at regular times so viewers know when to find you.
  • Greet new viewers by name and keep the conversation going.
  • Invest in simple lighting – a ring light makes a big difference.
  • Keep your profile photo fresh, clear and in line with the standards.
  • Use earphones and a quiet room for clear audio on calls.
  • Stay positive and respectful, even with difficult users.
  • Ask your agency for feedback and learn from top hosts.
FAQ

Chamet Host FAQs

Common questions from new Chamet hosts in India.

How do I register as a Chamet host in India?

Install the Chamet app, create one personal account, complete face verification and bind your own phone number or Gmail. Then message us on WhatsApp to get the current host registration link and join under an agency.

Who can become a Chamet host?

Adults aged 18 or older who use one personal account, can complete face verification, have a valid Metwallet account and agree to follow the platform's content and profile standards.

How do Chamet hosts earn?

Hosts receive Beans from eligible gifts during live streams and from private video calls. Beans are converted into a withdrawable balance at the platform's current rate. Earnings depend on activity, eligibility, performance and platform rules.

How many Beans make one dollar?

The Beans-to-USD rate is set by the platform and can change, so we do not publish a fixed figure. Check the current rate in your Chamet app, then use the Beans estimator on this page.

What is the minimum withdrawal for hosts?

According to the supplied platform presentation, the minimum withdrawal is $10, in multiples of $10. Any remainder carries over to your next withdrawal. Eligible withdrawals go to your bound Metwallet account.

Can I use more than one Chamet account?

No. Multiple accounts are prohibited and can lead to restrictions or suspension.

Do I need to show my face?

Yes. Face verification is required, and your face should be clearly visible during live streaming. Profiles with hidden or unclear faces may not be approved.

Can I change my agency later?

Switching agencies through improper solicitation is prohibited. If you have a genuine issue, contact the platform's support for guidance rather than moving accounts.

Is there any registration fee?

We do not charge for sharing information or the registration link. Be cautious of anyone who asks for money to guarantee approval or income.
