FONO App Download and Installation Guide

Finding the genuine FONO app, avoiding fake APK files, checking permissions and what to do right after installing.

Published 2 November 20265 min readBased on the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025

Before you can work as a FONO host, you need the FONO app on your phone. Because popular apps attract copycats, installing the genuine version safely matters. Fonoapp.in only publishes a download link once it has been verified – until then, use the steps below or ask us on WhatsApp for the current official link.

How to find the genuine FONO app

  1. Open the official app store on your phone, or use the official link shared by FONO.
  2. Search for “FONO”.
  3. Check the app name, publisher/developer and description carefully. Copycat apps often use similar names and icons.
  4. Read recent reviews and check the update date.

Why to avoid unknown APK files

APK files from random websites, social media posts or messaging groups can be modified versions of an app. They may contain malware, steal login details or simply not work with your account. If someone sends you an APK and says it is the “agency version”, ask for the official link instead.

Installing and setting up

Permissions

A video and audio calling app will reasonably ask for camera and microphone access. Be cautious if an app asks for permissions that have nothing to do with calls.

Registering as a host

After installing, register as a host. According to the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025, you do not need a profile picture or audition video, but you must enter your personal PAN card number. See how to become a Fono Host.

Registering under an agency

If an agency invited you, register under that agency only – the policy allows one agent per streamer.

After installing

  • Learn the call rates for each level.
  • Understand the withdrawal thresholds and timings.
  • Keep the app updated to get the latest features and fixes.
