How to Join Fono Agency in India?

Who the FONO agency model suits, what to prepare, how the 10% commission works and the recruitment rules you accept when you join.

Published 2 November 20266 min readBased on the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025

Joining Fono Agency in India means becoming a registered partner who builds a network of FONO streamers and receives commission on their earnings. Before you register, it helps to know whether the model fits you, what it pays and what it asks of you. This article answers those questions; for the click-by-click process, read the complete Fono Agency registration guide.

Who should join a Fono Agency?

The FONO agency model rewards people who can find and support streamers, not people who want to stream themselves. It tends to suit:

  • Community builders – people with a network of friends, students or job seekers who may want flexible online work.
  • Existing coordinators – anyone who already manages teams and is comfortable explaining rules and following up.
  • Patient planners – commission grows with the number of active streamers, so results build over time.

If you would rather earn directly from calls, Fono Hosting is the better route.

What a FONO agency earns

According to the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025, agencies receive a flat 10% commission on the eligible earnings of their registered streamers. There are no levels or tiers for agencies in the supplied policy – every agency gets the same rate.

Commission becomes withdrawable once it reaches 100,000 Beans. With a 10% rate, that means your streamers together need roughly 1,000,000 Beans of eligible earnings before your first agency withdrawal. The FONO agency commission guide walks through more examples.

What to prepare before you join

Understand the host side

Your streamers will ask you about call rates, PAN requirements and withdrawals. Learn the host earnings structure and the withdrawal policy first, so you can answer accurately.

Plan your recruitment

The supplied policy forbids recruiting streamers from other agents and existing hosts from other agencies. Your network must come from people who are new to FONO, so think about where they will come from before you register.

Accept the rules and penalties

Violations carry penalties of 100,000 Beans for a first offence and 500,000 Beans for a second, with commission deductions or termination for repeated violations. Read the FONO agency rules in full.

How to join, in brief

  1. Download the FONO app with the Join as Agency button on our Fono Agency page and register with referral code ALFVVDRU.
  2. Complete agency registration through the official process.
  3. Invite new streamers and help each one register under your agency with their own PAN card number.
  4. Support your streamers and keep your network compliant with platform guidelines.

Common questions from new agents

Do I need to pay to join?

The supplied policy does not mention any agency registration fee. Be careful with anyone who asks for money to “activate” an agency.

Can my streamers also work with another agent?

No. The policy allows only one agent per streamer at a time.

Is the commission guaranteed?

No. It depends on your streamers’ eligible earnings, and the platform calculates the final amount.

Actual earnings, commission and withdrawals are subject to platform eligibility and the rules in force. No income is guaranteed, and not every host reaches the higher call rates.
