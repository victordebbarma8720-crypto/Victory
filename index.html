Fono Agency

Fono Agency: Build a Streamer Network and Earn 10% Commission

A Fono Agency is a registered partner that recruits and manages a network of FONO streamers. Instead of earning from calls directly, the agency receives commission calculated on the eligible earnings of its registered streamers.

According to the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025, the agency commission is a flat 10%, and agency commission can be withdrawn once it reaches 100,000 Beans. Agencies must also follow recruitment rules that protect streamers and other agencies.

Agency Commission

FONO Agency Commission Overview

FONO uses a single, flat commission rate for agencies: 10% of the eligible earnings of the agency’s registered streamers. There are no tiers to unlock in the supplied policy – the rate is the same for every registered agency.

Agency commission becomes withdrawable once it reaches the minimum agency withdrawal of 100,000 Beans. Agencies that break recruitment rules can face Beans penalties, commission deductions or termination of cooperation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about FONO, registration, call rates, Beans and withdrawals.

What is FONO?

FONO is an anonymous live communication platform that offers video calls, audio calls and virtual gifts. Eligible streamers (hosts) earn Beans through these activities, and registered agencies receive commission based on their streamers’ earnings.

What is Fono Agency Registration?

It is the process of registering as an agency that builds and manages a network of FONO streamers. According to the FONO Policy dated 08 December 2025, registered agencies receive a flat 10% commission on their registered streamers’ eligible earnings. See the Fono Agency registration guide.

How do I register as a Fono Host?

The policy says no profile picture and no audition video are required, but you must provide your personal PAN card number. Read the Fono Hosting guide for the full requirements, then use the Join as Host button.

What are the FONO call rates?

Rates depend on your level. Video calls range from 100 Beans/min at Level 1 to 600 Beans/min at Level 6. Audio calls are 100 Beans/min for Levels 1–4, 150 Beans/min at Level 5 and 200 Beans/min at Level 6.

How much is 1,000 FONO Beans in INR?

Under the policy, 1,000 Beans = ₹10, so 100,000 Beans = ₹1,000. Our Beans to INR guide has more worked examples.

What is the minimum FONO withdrawal?

For streamers, the policy lists 5,000 Beans for the first withdrawal and 20,000 Beans for subsequent withdrawals, through self-withdrawal between 8 AM and 8 PM every day. Agencies need a minimum of 100,000 Beans to withdraw commission.

Why is a PAN card required?

The policy requires every host to provide their personal PAN card number. Incorrect PAN details may prevent future Bean withdrawals, so enter your own PAN exactly as it appears on the card.

Is fonoapp.in the official FONO website?

No. Fonoapp.in is an independent information resource. It explains FONO policies in plain language and helps visitors find the right registration route. Always confirm current rules in the FONO app.
