About Chamet

What Is Chamet and How Does It Work?

Chamet is a live video chat platform. Users discover hosts, watch live streams, send virtual gifts and start private one-to-one video calls. Hosts are verified adults who stream and take calls; the gifts and paid interactions they receive are credited as Beans.

The platform works through a host and agency system. Hosts (often called hostesses on the platform) join under an agency. The agency recruits hosts, helps them set up and understand the rules, and receives a separate commission from the platform. Agencies can also invite sub-agencies to build a larger team.

Chamet Agency India helps people in India understand this system and connects them with the current registration process through WhatsApp.

Hosts

Go live, take video calls, receive gifts and Beans, and withdraw eligible earnings to Metwallet.

Agencies

Recruit and support hosts, grow sub-agencies, and earn a commission ratio on eligible team earnings.

Agency Commission Program

How Chamet Agency Commission Works

Agency commission is calculated daily from the cashable earnings of your team.

Direct hostesses

Hosts registered directly under your agency. You earn your full commission ratio on their cashable earnings.

Sub-agencies

Agencies you invite. You earn the difference between your ratio and theirs on their team's cashable earnings.

Team performance

Your daily ratio depends on your whole team's eligible earnings over the recent 30 days.

Commission calculation

Direct: earnings × your ratio. Sub-agency: (your ratio − sub-agency ratio) × their earnings.

Daily commission ratios

The ratio is recalculated daily. New agents with less than 30 days are measured from registration to the previous day.

Recent 30-day earnings

A rolling total of eligible team earnings. A higher 30-day total can move your agency to a higher ratio.

Cash-out

Cash-Out Rules

How daily cash-out works for hosts and agents.

  • Yesterday's salary can be withdrawn under the platform's daily settlement process.
  • Minimum withdrawal amount: $10.
  • Withdrawals must be in multiples of $10.

Rules

Agency Rules and Hostess Rules

Following these rules protects your account, your earnings and your team.

Agency rules

  • Do not register hostesses who are already working with another Chamet agency.
  • Do not encourage Chamet hostesses to move to other applications.
  • Agencies must not deduct or withhold hostess payments.
  • Agency commissions are paid separately according to platform rules.
  • Follow all platform policies and instructions.

Hostess rules

  • Multiple accounts are prohibited.
  • Switching agencies through improper solicitation is prohibited.
  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content is prohibited.
  • Users under 18 are not eligible.
  • Policy violations may result in account restrictions, suspension or loss of related commissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Chamet, hosting, agencies and payments.

What is Chamet?

Chamet is a live video chat and live streaming app where users can watch live streams, chat, join private video calls and send virtual gifts. Hosts earn Beans from eligible activity, and agencies help recruit and support hosts.

How does the Chamet earning system work?

Hosts receive Beans from eligible activities such as live streaming gifts and private video calls. Beans are converted to a withdrawable balance at the platform's current rate. Agencies earn a separate commission based on their team's eligible earnings. Actual earnings depend on platform rules, activity, eligibility and performance.

What are Beans?

Beans are the in-app earning unit that hosts receive from gifts and eligible paid interactions. The platform converts Beans into a cash balance at its current conversion rate, which you should always check in the app because it can change.

How does agency commission work?

An agency earns a daily commission ratio on the cashable earnings of its direct hosts. For a sub-agency, the parent agency earns the difference between its own ratio and the sub-agency's ratio. For example, (22% - 18%) x $500 = $20. Ratios depend on eligible earnings over the recent 30 days.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

According to the supplied platform presentation, the minimum withdrawal is $10 and withdrawals are made in multiples of $10. Any remaining amount stays in your balance for the next withdrawal. Always confirm the current rules in the app.

What is Metwallet?

Metwallet is the wallet account used for Chamet settlements. Hosts and agents bind a valid Metwallet account, and the platform presentation states that eligible withdrawals reach the bound Metwallet in real time.

What are the requirements for Chamet hosts?

You must be 18 or older, use one personal account only, complete face verification, bind your own phone number or Gmail, have a valid Metwallet account and follow the platform's content and profile standards.

Can I join without experience?

Yes. You do not need previous streaming experience. You do need a smartphone, a stable internet connection, good lighting and a willingness to learn and follow the platform rules. We explain the basics when you contact us.
